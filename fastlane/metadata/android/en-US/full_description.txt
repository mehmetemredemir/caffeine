Caffeine keeps your screen on for a set time, indefinitely, or automatically
while a chosen app is in the foreground.

Features:
- Quick durations (5/10/15/30 min) or indefinite
- Custom duration with saved presets
- Quick Settings Tile with long-press preset picker
- Per-app wakelock (e.g. keep the screen on only while your e-reader is open)
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
